# Frankfurt 1965 MFH — Energy Retrofit Study

## Project

Energy performance and retrofit assessment of a representative 1965 multifamily residential building (Mehrfamilienhaus, MFH) in Frankfurt, Germany.

**Reference typology:** TABULA DE.N.MFH.05.Gen.ReEx.001

## Objective

The project develops a documented baseline-to-retrofit workflow for assessing the energy performance of an existing 1965 residential building.

The retrofit study targets substantial improvement in building energy performance, with GEG requirements and KfW/BEG guidance used as reference frameworks during the development of retrofit measures.

## Building

- Location: Frankfurt, Germany
- Building type: Multifamily residential building (MFH)
- Construction period: 1965
- Footprint: 16 × 12 m
- Number of floors: 4
- Floor-to-floor height: approximately 2.75 m
- Heated floor area: approximately 768 m²
- Main 16 m façades: north / south
- 12 m façades: east / west

## Technical Scope

The study currently includes:

- Existing-condition Revit model
- Frankfurt climate analysis using IWEC weather data
- Whole-building modelling in DesignBuilder / EnergyPlus
- Envelope and U-value assessment
- THERM 7.8 two-dimensional thermal-bridge analysis
- TABULA-based baseline comparison and validation
- Integration of calculated thermal-bridge values into the energy model

## Ongoing Development

The project is being developed progressively. Planned and ongoing stages include:

- Moisture and condensation assessment
- Retrofit envelope development
- Updated thermal-bridge assessment
- Retrofit energy simulation
- Baseline versus retrofit comparison
- Final technical documentation
